# Advanced Parametric Scandinavian Architecture

Expanding upon the initial tactile and parametric principles, this phase introduces highly detailed, localized architectural components that elevate the Scandinavian aesthetic to a more immersive, computational level.

## 1. Acoustic Diffusor Wall Paneling
The vast, raw concrete cylindrical gallery walls are now adorned with undulating wooden acoustic diffusor panels along the perimeter.
- **Parametric Generation**: Dozens of vertical timber slats are arrayed along the curve of the space. Their projection depths and heights vary based on overlapping mathematical waves, creating a visual rhythm that suggests sound waves frozen in material form.
- **Aesthetic Benefit**: Warms up the otherwise harsh concrete boundaries and provides a beautiful interplay of light and shadow, grounding the gallery in Nordic warmth.

## 2. Topographic Slatted Seating
The straightforward concrete and wood benches have been replaced by continuous topographic slatted benches.
- **Parametric Generation**: Constructed from parallel wooden ribs where the profile of each rib forms the ergonomic curve of a seat. The profile morphs smoothly along the length of the bench, creating an organic, terrain-like resting area.
- **Aesthetic Benefit**: Merges furniture directly into the architectural language of the sweeping ceiling canopy and acoustic baffles, making the entire space feel like a unified, computationally grown environment rather than individually placed objects.

## 3. Parametric Oculus Chandelier
Suspended delicately beneath the grand central sky-oculus is an intricate, parametric ribbed light fixture.
- **Parametric Generation**: A cascading series of wooden spiral ribs that fan outward and downward, mathematically arrayed to catch the pure white light from the sky-glow above.
- **Aesthetic Benefit**: Acts as a stunning focal point in the atrium, drawing the eye upward and emphasizing the towering volume of the museum space, while strictly maintaining the ash/birch timber palette.
