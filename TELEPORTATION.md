# Meta Quest 3 Teleportation & Hand Controls

Welcome to the spatial gallery teleportation and interaction system, fully supported for the **Meta Quest 3** hand controllers! This short guide details how to navigate and interact with garments in immersive VR mode.

## Using the Teleportation System

Teleportation allows you to fluidly move around the massive, hyper-scale gallery environment and get up close to every piece without causing motion sickness.

### How to Teleport
1. **Aim:** Point your physical Meta Quest 3 controller toward the floor or the center runway stage.
2. **Press:** Squeeze and hold the **Grip Button** (the button under your middle finger). A blue laser curve will originate from your controller and illuminate a landing spot on the floor, indicated by a red target ring.
3. **Move:** Release the **Grip Button** to instantly blink to the targeted location. 

### Teleportation Zones
You can teleport to any functional floor surface within the environment, including:
- The main concrete gallery floor.
- The extended center black-lit runway (look for the illuminated light strips).
- Right next to the exhibition stands, getting close to inspect the futuristic garments.

## Interacting with the Exhibitions

The gallery isn't just visually distinct—it's highly interactive. While wandering the space, you can customize the historical and futuristic exhibits.

### Hand & Laser Interactions
- **Focus Haptics:** Pointing your controller laser at any button or interactive item on the hovering UI panels will trigger a subtle haptic vibration pulse directly in your controller's handle to help confirm your focus and target point.
- **Select / Click:** Point your controller at an interactive UI panel hovering near a garment and pull the **Trigger** (the index finger button) to activate buttons.
- **Grab to Inspect:** If you use the "Inspect" button to bring the garment close to you, you can then press and hold the **Trigger** on the garment itself to grab, manually rotate, and freely examine the 3D model up close with your controllers.
- **Parametric Sliders:** Click and drag the sliders on the right side of the UI panel with your **Trigger** to dynamically tweak lighting glow (GLOW) or the rotation speed (ROTATE).

## Desktop Controls Override
If you decide to preview the project without your Quest headset on a normal 2D browser:
- **Look:** Click and drag the **Left Mouse Button** on the empty background.
- **Move:** Use **W, A, S, D** or the **Arrow Keys**, just like standard first-person navigational engines.
- **Interact:** Click directly on the garments' UI panels to interact.

Enjoy the spatial experience!
